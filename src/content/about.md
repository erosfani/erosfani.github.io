I am currently pursuing research on **Mixture-of-Experts** and **Model Merging** for **Large Language Models**. My research focuses on **building scalable, distributed models** over uniquely **decentralised and heterogeneous infrastructure**, and on addressing the challenge of **data heterogeneity** in real-world applications of **federated learning**. Additionally, I possess a solid track record in **semantic segmentation**, **domain adaptation**, **domain generalization**, **semi-supervised learning**.

Previously, I was a **Machine Learning Researcher** at **Gensyn**, a **Postdoctoral Fellow** at the **Basque Center for Applied Mathematics**, and a [**Senior Researcher** in the Predictive Analytics Lab at the **University of Sussex**](https://wearepal.ai/team). I have earned a **Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence** at the **Visual and Multimodal Applied Learning Laboratory** within the Artificial Intelligence Hub at the **Polytechnic University of Turin** (thesis title: [**Addressing Heterogeneity in Federated Learning for Real-world Vision Applications**](./phd_thesis_eros_fani.pdf)). My Ph.D. was conducted within the framework of the [European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems (**ELLIS**) **Ph.D. program**](https://ellis.eu/student/2021-eros-fani), a pan-European initiative for excellence in machine learning and AI.

I have published and presented my work at several international peer-reviewed conferences and workshops, including **ICML**, **ICLR**, **NeurIPS**, **CVPR**, **IROS**, and **WACV**, and to an **IEEE** journal (check my publications [here](#research)). Furthermore, I have served as a reviewer for many international conferences and journals, and I was honored as an [**Outstanding Reviewer**](certificate_outstanding_reviewer_ECCV24.pdf) at **ECCV** in 2024.

I am always driven by curiosity and have a strong interest in exploring new ideas and challenges within and beyond my current research. Open to research and engineering roles in Machine Learning.
