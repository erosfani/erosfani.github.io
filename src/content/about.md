
I am an **ML Research Scientist** with expertise in **LLMs Pre- and Post-Training**, **Parameter-Efficient Fine-Tuning**, **Mixture-of-Experts**, **Model Merging**, **LLM Routing**, **Decentralized** and **Federated Learning**. I also possess a solid track record in **Semantic Segmentation**, **Domain Adaptation**, **Domain Generalization**, and **Semi-Supervised Learning**. My Ph.D. was conducted within the framework of the [European Laboratory for Learning and Intelligent Systems (**ELLIS**) **Ph.D. program**](https://ellis.eu/student/2021-eros-fani), a pan-European initiative for excellence in machine learning and AI.
